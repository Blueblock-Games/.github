## Our Games

### Little City
*Build Your City. Shape Your World.*

Little City is a creative city-building game where players can design, build, and manage their own cities.

- **Build** houses, roads, schools, hospitals, parks, and more.
- **Manage** your city's economy and earn money over time.
- **Expand** your city and unlock new possibilities.
- **Collaborate** with friends through multiplayer city building.
- **Create** your own unique city, from a small town to a bustling metropolis.

More games are coming in the future!
